Combined portfolio now includes over 50 dispensaries and 10 cultivation and production facilities across eight U.S. states CHICAGO & DENVER--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- PharmaCann Inc. , one of the nation’s largest privately held, vertically integrated cannabis companies, and LivWell Enlightened Health , a multi-state cannabis cultivation and retail leader based in Colorado, today announced the completion of their previously disclosed definitive merger agreement for PharmaCann to acquire LivWell Holdings, Inc.
