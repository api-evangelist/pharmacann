CHICAGO (April 24, 2023) – Cannabis consumers with a taste for adventure now have a new option: P3 infused pre-rolls. Featuring a glass tip filter for less clogging as well as irresistible flavors and scents, each P3 pre-roll is infused with cannabis distillate and finished off with unique combinations of cannabis and botanical terpenes. P3 infused pre-rolls are now available at all Verilife dispensary locations in Illinois.
