CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- PharmaCann Inc. (“PharmaCann” or the “Company”), one of the largest, private, vertically integrated cannabis companies in the United States, today announced that it has completed an additional issuance of 12% Senior Secured Notes due June 30, 2025 (the “Notes”). The net proceeds from the upsize were approximately $39.5 million, after the original issue discount and other estimated offering expenses, and will be used for strategic growth opportunities and general corporate purposes.
