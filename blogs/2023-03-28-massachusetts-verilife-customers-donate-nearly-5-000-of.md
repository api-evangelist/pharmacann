WAREHAM, Mass. (March 28, 2023) – Customers of Verilife dispensaries in Massachusetts reached into their pockets during the last two quarters of 2022 to donate the spare change from their dispensary purchases to Turning Point Wareham (Wareham Area Committee for the Homeless – WACH), a nonprofit organization that provides services and assistance to unhoused and near-homeless populations.
