V erilife Celebrates the Grand Opening of its Seventh Pennsylvania Dispensary on November 23 PHILADELPHIA-- – (November 22, 2021) – PharmaCann Inc. , one of the nation’s largest privately held cannabis companies, today announced the opening of its newest Verilife medical marijuana dispensary in Lancaster, PA, on Tuesday, November 23. “We are pleased to become an active member of the Lancaster community with the opening of our newest dispensary," said Bill McMenamy, President of Verilife.
